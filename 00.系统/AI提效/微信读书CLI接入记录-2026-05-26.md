## 接入后可做的事

- 查看书架：`weread shelf list`
- 最近阅读：`weread shelf recent --limit 10`
- 搜书：`weread search "书名" --scope book`
- 导出笔记：`weread notes export <bookId> --format markdown --output notes.md`
- 读取统计：`weread readdata summary --mode monthly`
