# Daily AI Operating System

## Morning
1. List active projects, deadlines, blockers, and energy level.
2. Pick 1 deep-work target, 3 support tasks, and 1 knowledge asset to create.
3. Assign AI roles: researcher, coder, reviewer, writer, organizer.

## During Work
- Convert repeated actions into prompts, templates, scripts, or skills.
- Capture decisions and reusable rules in Obsidian.

## Evening
Summarize output, lessons, reusable artifacts, unresolved blockers, and tomorrow's first action.
